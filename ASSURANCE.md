# Assurance

This records where `tacenta-core` stands against the expectations in
[ADR-0008](tacenta-spec/decisions/ADR-0008-assurance-expectations.md), and what
comes next. It is a summary. For what is proven, `tacenta-proofs/CLAIMS.md` and
`LIMITATIONS.md` are the record. For what the vectors pin,
`tacenta-test-vectors/conformance-manifest.md` is. For current gate and target
obligations, see [ASSURANCE-OBLIGATIONS.md](ASSURANCE-OBLIGATIONS.md).

Last assessed: 2026-09-30, at `dea57eaf`, which is 28 commits after the
assessment of 2026-09-25 at `bba8f04`. Practices 1 to 10, the Rust expectations,
the Roadmap, the Components table and the readiness section were each read again
against the tree at `dea57eaf`, and the counts they quote were recomputed from
the tree, except that the Lean build lines in `tacenta-proofs/REPRODUCING.md` are read from
the log of the `translation` job for `dea57eaf`. The mutation counts in `ASSURANCE-OBLIGATIONS.md` are from the record in
`tacenta-proofs/GATE-MUTATION-RECORD.md`, except the cases of `check-session-satisfiability-negatives.sh`, which are those of the script at this tree. The reads were tool-assisted (see the next paragraph) and were not
made by anyone independent of the maintainer. This assessment did not
run every acceptance criterion of every closed gap row. `GAP-REGISTER.md` and
`ASSURANCE-OBLIGATIONS.md` carry their own stamps. The pin counts in practice 2, the Session row, the readiness section and the Gate 2 count, and the trust-base sentences beside them, are those of the tree after #215, the `SessionBraidReceiveVacuity` change, the repair of the decoder hypothesis, the inhabitation modules and the `DispatchEvidenceVacuity` change, not of `dea57eaf`. Pull request #210 (a
specification page) merged after `dea57eaf`, and neither it nor pull request #211
is covered by the assessment. The closure of `E2E-07` (#214) was made afterwards and is covered only by its register row. In this record, "hosted" means run by GitHub
Actions. On a push to `main`, the `proofs` and `translation` jobs run on the
project's self-hosted runners and every other job on GitHub-hosted machines; a
pull request's `proofs` and `translation` jobs run on GitHub-hosted machines and
replay the messages that `main`'s build logged for unchanged modules.

Parts of this project are written and reviewed with tool assistance. The
README's "Development assistance" section says how, and that tool-assisted
checks and reviews are evidence of what they ran, not independent review.

target. Gate 2 is not met: five rows are open at BLOCKING in `GAP-REGISTER.md`

tacenta.com home page says "proven core"), and INV-01 is open at AMBIGUOUS. Gates 3 and 4 have not been met either; see the readiness section.

**Notes on the levels.**

- The L4 rows for the ratchets and the Triple Ratchet, and the Braid's L4 target, are success-side refinements. Their theorems take the premises `CLAIMS.md` lists: a step of counter headroom, bounds on store and chain sizes, bounds on kept epochs and, for the Braid receive, a bound on the stored KEM ciphertext (`ct1_bounded`), on the session unit also a bound on the erasure decoders (`decoders_bounded`), and the live-encoder and unspliced-stream conditions ([PROOF-BOUNDARY-HEADROOM-TARGET-DECISION.md](tacenta-proofs/PROOF-BOUNDARY-HEADROOM-TARGET-DECISION.md); `GAP-REGISTER.md` row HL-FM-04).
- The Braid row reads L3. It was set there because its receive refinements take `ct1_bounded` as a premise, and on the session unit also `decoders_bounded`, and no theorem showed that a send keeps either. Preservation theorems for the Braid's `send`, `receive` and constructors now exist, on the standalone translation and on the session unit, and for a decoded Braid on the unit (`CLAIMS.md`, "Proved (the ML-KEM Braid keeps the two premises its receive theorems take)" and the two sections after it). They assume one law about the erasure decoder on the standalone translation, and on the session unit two laws about `Vec::truncate` and `usize::div_ceil` (`LIMITATIONS.md`, "The Braid's preservation theorems"), and have had no independent read. ADR-0008 reads L4 as L3 plus T3 refinement with the stated invariants proved. This table does not change the label; moving it is a decision for the maintainer. `CLAIMS.md` ("What is not claimed: preservation") records no preservation theorem for the classical and sparse ratchets either, and this table keeps those rows at L4; no recorded decision says why. ADR-0008 defines levels L1 to L4 only, and an earlier version of this row read "L3/L4". An earlier pull request changed that label to the lower level the evidence supports. That change is not a recorded decision, and whether the ratchet rows keep L4 is undecided. The Triple Ratchet row rests on four size preconditions that no theorem discharges (`tacenta-proofs/README.md`, Status); this table keeps it at L4 for the same undecided reason.
- The Session row's five T1 theorems take contract records that hold 15 to 45 named contract hypotheses each, plus a headroom record. Three of them also take the class `SessionUnitT1.DerivedKeysModel`. None of the six depends on a compiler-trust axiom, and each carries an axiom pin. The pins fix which axioms each theorem depends on and say nothing about whether the records can be met. `LIMITATIONS.md` records that the four records follow from an axiom base that one interpretation satisfies, under five laws about standard-library and `zeroize` operations. That is an argument about derivations. Their headroom records are satisfiable in the same sense, with the inhabitedness of the opaque types (one of them, the key pair, an assumption) and two further assumptions in the base (`LIMITATIONS.md`, "The headroom records"). Two of them, `DecryptRatchetContracts` and `EstablishResponderContracts`, contained `BraidReceiveContracts` with a false field, so `decrypt_no_panic`, `decrypt_ratchet_no_panic` and `establish_responder_no_panic` held vacuously as stated, as did the session unit's Braid receive theorems that took the same field; the field is now stated for decoders that need at most `MAX_CODEWORDS` chunks (`GAP-REGISTER.md`, row `SESSION-CONTRACT-VACUITY`). `CLAIMS.md` gives the per-theorem counts.

- the security properties are proved against a symbolic attacker, not a

- the Braid's `key_pair` content clause is scoped to implementations that know
  the delegated KEM layout, and no vector can pin it;
- session orchestration is modelled, pinned and, since the Phase 0 carve-out,
  translated with conditional T1 theorems only, so ASM-19 carries it. Thirteen
  requirements rest on ASM-19 in `evidence-index.json`: eleven are "tested only",
  one is "assumed", and one (`REQ-CONF-02`) is "proved" with
  `session-orchestration-proof` listed as missing evidence. Two further proved
  requirements, `REQ-AUTH-08` and `REQ-AUTH-14`, record a missing session-layer
  proof that points at ASM-19.

Gate 3 is deliberately the last thing done before the second engagement, because
it is only true of the ledger as it stands on the day.
