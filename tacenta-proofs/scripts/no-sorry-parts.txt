# The parts of a split run of no-sorry.sh: `<partition> <part>: <phase>[@k/S] ...`, or `*` for every phase.
#
# Every part also runs the prelude (the translation, proofs and model builds and their scans), which every
# other phase reads. Each partition must give each other phase of the script to exactly one part, or each
# slice k/S of a sliceable phase to exactly one part; `scripts/no-sorry-parts.py` refuses this file
# otherwise, and refuses it when the script has a phase that no part of a partition names. Part names are
# unique over the file; CI names its matrix jobs and their artifacts after them.
#
# `hosted` is what a pull request runs, one part per GitHub-hosted job. The groups are made so that each
# job's phases took at most about nine minutes in a hosted run of the whole script (run 39533849612), before the
# `reply` part took on `send-preservation` and `initiator-second-send`; its five phases took 340 s locally and are
# estimated at about eleven to thirteen minutes hosted (an estimate, not a hosted measurement); the
# `responder` part then took on `responder-second-receipt`, whose control alone took about 310 s locally under
# `nice` (not a hosted measurement); the initial dispatcher's 50 controls took 3024 s there and are split eight
# ways. The `braid` part later took on `responder-second-send`, whose control took between about 85 s and 105 s in
# local runs under `nice` (not a hosted measurement). The `reply` part later took on `initiator-repeated-reply`: in one
# sequential local run under `nice` on 2026-10-23 its six phases' controls took about 510 s in all (the times of the
# separate phases were not kept); the hosted time is not re-estimated and, by the ratio above, may be well over the nine
# minutes this part was made for.
